# Foldnote overview

Foldnote is a visual note-taking app for iPhone by Ruzname Labs.

## What Foldnote is

Foldnote helps people create, organize, and share notes using:
- structured blocks
- folders
- stickers
- reminders
- shared spaces and groups

It is built for personal planning and lightweight collaboration.

## Core use cases

- weekly planning and errands
- shared household notes and lists
- travel planning
- routines and habit tracking
- study notes and organized reference material
- recipes, checklists, and everyday life admin

## Product positioning

Foldnote is designed to feel warmer and more visual than a plain text note app. The interface emphasizes clarity, structure, and personality through blocks, paper styles, color, and stickers.
