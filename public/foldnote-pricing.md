# Foldnote pricing

Foldnote offers a free entry point and premium subscription options through the Apple App Store.

## Free access

Free users can try the product and explore the core note-taking experience before upgrading.

## Premium summary

Premium expands the product with:
- unlimited personal notes
- larger groups
- more visual customization

## Public pricing summary

- Free plan available
- Yearly premium plan: USD 19.99
- Yearly plan includes a 2-week free trial

For the most current storefront-specific pricing and subscription presentation, use the official App Store listing:

- https://apps.apple.com/us/app/foldnote/id6761731923

## Purchase context

Foldnote uses Apple App Store auto-renewable subscriptions for premium access.
